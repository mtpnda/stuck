Stuck is a minimalist Scandinavian cognitive load manager and capacity balancer designed to bring serenity to your workflow when your mind feels overloaded.

When too many thoughts, open loops, and pending decisions pile up, you feel stuck. Stuck gives you a calm, tactile mental container to externalize what is in your head, balance your cognitive load, and move with clarity.

KEY CAPABILITIES:

1. Cognitive Load Balancing
Set your maximum active headspace capacity (default 3 active contexts). When you attempt to juggle more than your set capacity, Stuck highlights the overload in alert coral, guiding you to park or close existing contexts before taking on more.

2. 4-Stage Thought Flow
Move contexts through their natural life cycle:
• Coming Up: Queued for later or tomorrow.
• In Your Head: Actively holding your focus today.
• Parked: Safely written down and cleared from your headspace.
• Closed: Resolved with focus time and metrics preserved.

3. Signature Stack-of-Bars Visual Architecture
A visual stack of cognitive bars gives you an instant, calm gauge of your mental bandwidth. Green calm sage signifies focused presence, while coral highlights cognitive overload.

4. Built-in Focus Sessions
Tap "Work on" on any active context to start a distraction-free focus timer. Track how much undivided attention you dedicate to each thought.

5. 100% Zero-Cloud Privacy
All data is stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage. No remote servers, no tracking, and no external analytics.

6. Fast BioCue Authentication
Instant 1-tap Guest demo mode, Google Sign-In, and local biometric security (Face ID & Fingerprint) for instant access.
